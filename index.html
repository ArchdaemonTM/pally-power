Step I — Identity

Who Are You?

Every Paladin begins with a name and a story. In the modern era, your Paladin identity is who you actually are. This is the LUMINOUS engine: your real life is the campaign.

You are ordained the moment you choose to stand for something. This profile records your answer. Every field below accepts custom input — your path is yours to define.

Step II — Color Order

Choose Your Order

The Color Orders represent the full spectrum of Paladin philosophy. From pure spiritual to pure martial. Your Order determines available Callings and your meta-category.

Step III — Alignment

Your Moral Compass

Your alignment determines how you pursue your oath — not whether your oath is "good." Modern Paladins exist across the full moral spectrum.

Step IV — Attributes

Core Attributes

Distribute 32 points across your attributes (min 8, max 18 at creation). Like Elder Scrolls, these grow through justified real-life action. You can also add custom attributes specific to your path.

Step V — Calling

Your Primary Calling

Your Calling is your primary role — the core identity that defines your approach. Select from your Order's callings or create a custom one.

Step VI — Specialization

Modern Specialization

Your real-world expertise. A Paladin who cannot function in the modern world is a cosplayer, not a champion. Select from existing specs or create your own.

Step VII — Talent Trees

Invest Your Talents

5 talent points at Level 1. +2 per level thereafter. Allocate across four trees. Sequential unlock — each node requires all previous nodes.

Step VIII — Level Plan

Your 50-Level Arc

Every 5 levels you earn a Feat. Capstones at 10/20/30/40/50. Skills require justified real-life growth. You choose when to level up.

Community Expansion

Suggest an Addition

The Paladin Profile system is designed to grow. Suggest new attributes, callings, specializations, talents, or even entirely new Color Orders. Approved suggestions become part of the engine for everyone.

This is how we build a culture together. Your suggestion will be reviewed and, if approved, added to the system for all Paladins to use.

The Dream Tracker

Level-Up Journal

Players choose when they level up. Each level must be earned through real growth in your real life. Describe what you accomplished, who mentored you, and what evidence you can point to.

To level up, you need your Character ID and Edit Token (given when you saved). Enter them below, then describe the growth that justifies your advancement.
